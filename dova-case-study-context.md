# CMS Structure

Collections:
- News posts - Company updates, new publications etc - rendered on 'News & Events' page
- Events - upcoming healthcare events where key figures in the company will be attending - rendered on 'News & Events' page
- Publications, Posters and Journal Articles - rendered on 'Publications' page
- Team members - rendered on 'About' page
- Testimonials - rendered on 'Partners' page
- Board of Directors - rendered on 'About page'
- Scientific Advisory Board - rendered on 'About page'



Goals:
- Scalable publishing
- Easy client updates
- Modular section reuse
